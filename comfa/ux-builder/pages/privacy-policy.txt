<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>Comfa respects the privacy of everyone who visits or shops with us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it and the choices you have. Questions are welcome at <a href="mailto:privacy@example.com">privacy@example.com</a>.</p>
<h3>What This Policy Covers</h3>
<p>It covers information we receive when you browse this website, create an account, place an order, sign up for emails or contact our team. "Personal information" means information that can identify or reach you, such as your name, email address, phone number or delivery address.</p>
<h3>What This Policy Does Not Cover</h3>
<p>It does not cover companies we do not control, such as payment providers, carriers and other sites we link to. They handle your information under their own policies, which we encourage you to read.</p>
<h3>Where Your Information Is Processed</h3>
<p>Comfa is operated by [Company legal name], [country]. By using the store you understand that your information is stored and processed there.</p>
<h2 id="how-we-collect-information">How We Collect Information</h2>
<h3>Information You Provide</h3>
<h4>When you create an account</h4>
<p>Your name, email address and password. You can add delivery addresses and a phone number later.</p>
<h4>When you place an order</h4>
<p>Your name, billing and delivery address, email address, phone number and the items you bought.</p>
<h4>When you sign up for emails</h4>
<p>Your email address and the date you signed up.</p>
<h4>When you ask for help</h4>
<p>Your contact details and whatever you include in your message, such as an order number or photos.</p>
<h3>Other Information We Collect</h3>
<h4>Log data and analytics</h4>
<p>Like most websites, our servers record technical details such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and the time of each visit. We use them to keep the store running and to understand what is popular.</p>
<h4>Cookies</h4>
<p>Cookies keep items in your bag, keep you signed in and remember your preferences. You can block cookies in your browser, but the bag and checkout will not work without them.</p>
<h4>Do Not Track</h4>
<p>Browsers send "Do Not Track" signals in different ways and there is no common standard, so the store does not respond to them at this time.</p>
<h3>Information We Do Not Collect</h3>
<h4>Children</h4>
<p>The store is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.</p>
<h4>Payment card numbers</h4>
<p>Card payments are handled by the payment provider you choose at checkout. We never see or store your full card number.</p>
<h2 id="how-we-use-information">How We Use Information</h2>
<h3>To Fulfil Your Order</h3>
<p>We use your information to take payment, pack and deliver your order, handle returns and answer your questions.</p>
<h3>In Our Communications With You</h3>
<h4>Service messages</h4>
<p>Order confirmations, delivery updates and account notices. These are part of your purchase and cannot be switched off.</p>
<h4>Promotional emails</h4>
<p>News and offers, sent only if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.</p>
<h3>To Improve the Store</h3>
<p>We look at combined, non-identifying figures, such as which products are viewed most, to improve our range and the website.</p>
<h2 id="how-we-share-and-disclose-information">How We Share and Disclose Information</h2>
<h3>With Service Providers</h3>
<p>We share only what is needed with companies that help us run the store: payment providers, carriers, email and hosting services. They may use it only to do that work for us.</p>
<h3>We Do Not Sell Your Information</h3>
<p>We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.</p>
<h3>Linked Services</h3>
<p>If you follow a link to another website or social network, that service's own privacy policy applies.</p>
<h3>As Necessary In Certain Legal Circumstances</h3>
<h4>Legal requirements</h4>
<p>We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Comfa, our customers or others.</p>
<h4>Business transfer</h4>
<p>If Comfa is merged or sold, customer information may pass to the new owner, who must continue to honour this policy.</p>
<h4>Your California privacy rights</h4>
<p>California residents may ask once a year which categories of personal information we shared with third parties for their direct marketing. We do not share information for that purpose.</p>
<h2 id="how-we-keep-information-secure">How We Keep Information Secure</h2>
<h3>Security Of Account Information</h3>
<p>Your account is protected by your password. Choose one you do not use elsewhere and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.</p>
<h3>Network Security</h3>
<p>Pages that send personal information are served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.</p>
<h3>Management Procedures</h3>
<p>Only team members who need customer information to do their job can access it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.</p>
<h2 id="your-personal-choices">Your Personal Choices</h2>
<h3>Access To Personal Information</h3>
<p>You can see your details and past orders in <a href="{{url:/my-account/}}">My Account</a>, or ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.</p>
<h3>Receiving Communications</h3>
<p>Unsubscribe from promotional emails with the link in any email. Order and account messages will still be sent.</p>
<h3>Editing Or Deleting Your Account</h3>
<p>Edit your details in My Account at any time. To delete your account, <a href="{{url:/contact/}}">contact us</a>. We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.</p>
<h3>Opting Out Of Advertising</h3>
<p>You can limit interest-based advertising in your browser or device settings.</p>
<h2 id="updates-to-our-privacy-policy">Updates to Our Privacy Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, and we will tell you by email or on the website about changes that affect how we use your information. Last updated: [date].</p>
