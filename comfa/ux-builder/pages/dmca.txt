<p>Comfa designs and sells its own tees, caps, hoodies and socks, and we respect the intellectual property rights of others, including copyright, trademark and related rights. If you believe that something on this website, such as a product design, a photo or a customer review, infringes your rights, please tell us using the procedure below.</p>
<h2 id="reporting-intellectual-property-infringement">A. Reporting Intellectual Property Infringement</h2>
<p>When we receive a complete notice, our policy is to (1) remove or block access to any content, including text, graphics and photos, that we believe in good faith infringes the rights of others, and (2) stop working with repeat infringers.</p>
<p>To report infringement, fill in the <a href="{{url:/ip-claim/}}">Intellectual Property Claim Form</a>, or send a notice with the following information to the Designated Agent listed at the end of this page:</p>
<ol>
<li>The copyrighted work, trademark or other intellectual property you believe has been infringed, with any registration numbers.</li>
<li>The content you believe infringes it, including (a) how it uses your work in a way that infringes, and (b) where it appears on this website, for example the page address, in enough detail for us to find it.</li>
<li>Your full name, mailing address, telephone number and email address.</li>
<li>A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agents or the law.</li>
<li>A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or are authorised to act for the rights holder.</li>
<li>The electronic or physical signature of the person authorised to act for the rights holder.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="once-a-valid-notification-is-received">B. Once a Valid Notification Is Received</h2>
<p>When the Designated Agent receives a complete, good-faith notice, our policy is:</p>
<ul>
<li>to remove or disable access to the content named in the notice;</li>
<li>to tell the person who supplied or posted that content, such as a design partner or reviewer, that it has been removed; and</li>
<li>to remove the content of repeat infringers and end their access to the website.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="supplying-a-counter-notice">C. Supplying a Counter-Notice</h2>
<p>If your content was removed and you believe it does not infringe, or that you have the right to use it from the rights holder or under the law (including fair use), send a counter-notice with the following information to the Designated Agent:</p>
<ol>
<li>The content that was removed or disabled, and where it appeared on this website before it was removed.</li>
<li>A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith the content was removed because of a mistake or misidentification.</li>
<li>Your full name, mailing address, telephone number and email address.</li>
<li>A statement that you accept the jurisdiction of the courts for the district where your address is, or, if you live outside [country], where Comfa is located, and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.</li>
<li>Your electronic or physical signature.</li>
</ol>
<p>When we receive a counter-notice we may send a copy to the person who made the original complaint and tell them that the content may be restored in 10 business days. Unless the rights holder files a court action against you, the content may be restored 10 to 14 business days or more after we receive the counter-notice, at our discretion.</p>
<p>Please note that under Section 512(f) of the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act, a person who knowingly misrepresents that content is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages, including legal fees.</p>
