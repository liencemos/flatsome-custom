<h2 id="before-you-place-your-order">Before you place your order</h2>
[accordion]
[accordion-item title="How much does shipping cost?"]
<p>Standard shipping is a flat $6. Shipping is free on orders over $75 after discounts.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How long will it take to ship my order?"]
<p>Orders leave our warehouse within one business day and usually arrive in 3–5 business days.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How do packs work?"]
<p>Packs bundle several of the same style at a lower price per item. Pick one colour and size for the whole pack.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How do I find my size?"]
<p>Every product page has a Size Guide link next to the size options, or see the full <a href="{{url:/size-guide/}}">Size Guide</a>.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="I am having issues placing an order."]
<p>Sorry about that. <a href="{{url:/contact/}}">Contact us</a> with what you were trying to buy and any error message, and we will sort it out.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
<h2 id="order-status-questions">Order status questions</h2>
[accordion]
[accordion-item title="What is the status of my order?"]
<p>We email you when your order is confirmed and again when it ships. If you have an account, your orders are listed in <a href="{{url:/my-account/}}">My Account</a>.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="I need help with a late order."]
<p>If your order has not arrived 10 business days after it shipped, <a href="{{url:/contact/}}">contact us</a> with your order number and we will chase the carrier.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="My tracking number is not working."]
<p>Tracking can take 1–2 days to appear in the carrier's system. If it still shows nothing after that, let us know.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
<h2 id="modifying-or-changing-an-order">Modifying or changing an order</h2>
[accordion]
[accordion-item title="I would like to modify my order."]
<p>Contact us within one hour of ordering and we will do our best to update the address or items before it ships.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="I would like to cancel my order."]
<p>We can cancel an order that has not shipped yet. Once it is on its way, you can return it within 60 days.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="My order is missing or defective."]
<p>We will put it right. <a href="{{url:/contact/}}">Contact us</a> with your order number and a photo of the problem, and we will send a replacement or a refund.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
<h2 id="payment">Payment</h2>
[accordion]
[accordion-item title="What payment methods do you accept?"]
<p>The payment methods available for your order are listed at checkout.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How secure is my payment?"]
<p>Checkout runs over an encrypted connection and card payments are processed by the payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Is my card charged as soon as I order?"]
<p>Yes, card payments are taken when you place the order. With cash on delivery you pay when the parcel arrives.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
<h2 id="shipping-returns-and-exchanges">Shipping, Returns and Exchanges</h2>
[accordion]
[accordion-item title="What is your return policy?"]
<p>If you are not happy, send it back within 60 days for a refund or an exchange. See the <a href="{{url:/return-refund-policy/}}">Return &amp; Refund Policy</a>.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How long does a refund or exchange take?"]
<p>We answer return requests within 3 business days. Refunds can take 5–10 business days to show on your statement.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How should I wash my tee or cap?"]
<p>Machine wash tees cold and tumble dry low. Spot clean caps and let them air dry to keep their shape.</p>
[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
