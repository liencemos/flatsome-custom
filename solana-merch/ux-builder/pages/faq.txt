[ux_text]<div class="edit-help-section" id="before-ordering"><p class="edit-label">01 / FAQ</p><h2>Before ordering</h2></div>[/ux_text]
[accordion class="edit-page-accordion"]
[accordion-item title="How much does shipping cost?"]<p>US standard shipping is $6 and is free from $75 after discounts. Checkout confirms the options for your address.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="When will my order ship?"]<p>Look for a dispatch email once your order is on the way. Product pages show the current business-day delivery estimate.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Why can’t I place my order?"]<p>Make sure you have selected a size and colour, then check the delivery and payment fields. If checkout still fails, <a href="{{contact_url}}">tell the studio</a> what happened without sending any payment details.</p>[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
[ux_text]<div class="edit-help-section" id="order-status"><p class="edit-label">02 / FAQ</p><h2>Order status</h2></div>[/ux_text]
[accordion class="edit-page-accordion"]
[accordion-item title="Where can I check my order?"]<p>Use the confirmation email or <a href="{{account_url}}">Your account</a>. Contact us with your order number if you checked out as a guest.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="My order is late. What should I do?"]<p>Check tracking first. If the delivery estimate has passed, send us the order number so we can check with the carrier.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Why isn’t my tracking number working?"]<p>Tracking can take time to update after a shipping label is created. If there is still no scan after a business day, contact the studio.</p>[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
[ux_text]<div class="edit-help-section" id="order-changes"><p class="edit-label">03 / FAQ</p><h2>Order changes</h2></div>[/ux_text]
[accordion class="edit-page-accordion"]
[accordion-item title="Can I change my order?"]<p>Contact the studio promptly. A size, colour or address change may be possible before packing. See the <a href="{{size_guide_url}}">Size Guide</a> when choosing a replacement size.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I cancel?"]<p>Send your order number as soon as you decide. If the order has shipped, follow the return instructions instead.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What if an item is missing or defective?"]<p>Tell us what was missing or damaged. Include your order number and a photo of the item or packaging if available.</p>[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
[ux_text]<div class="edit-help-section" id="payment"><p class="edit-label">04 / FAQ</p><h2>Payment</h2></div>[/ux_text]
[accordion class="edit-page-accordion"]
[accordion-item title="Which payment methods can I use?"]<p>Checkout shows current payment methods. The demo uses Cash on Delivery.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How do I pay securely?"]<p>Only use checkout to pay. We do not ask for card details or passwords through support.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What is a card security code?"]<p>A CVV or CVC helps verify a card payment. If a live payment provider needs it, enter it in checkout, not in a message.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="When is my card charged?"]<p>The demo does not charge cards. If a live card option is enabled, checkout shows when authorisation and payment occur.</p>[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
[ux_text]<div class="edit-help-section" id="shipping-returns"><p class="edit-label">05 / FAQ</p><h2>Shipping, returns &amp; exchanges</h2></div>[/ux_text]
[accordion class="edit-page-accordion"]
[accordion-item title="What is your return policy?"]<p>Start with the <a href="{{return_refund_policy_url}}">Return &amp; Refund Policy</a> and contact us before mailing an item.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How long do replacements and refunds take?"]<p>We confirm the next step after reviewing your order. Timing depends on stock, return inspection and your payment method.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="When will my order ship?"]<p>Look for a dispatch email. The delivery window on the product page is an estimate in business days.</p>[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How long does delivery take after dispatch?"]<p>The estimated US delivery window is 3–5 business days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed time in transit. See <a href="{{shipping_returns_url}}">Shipping &amp; Returns</a>.</p>[/accordion-item]
[/accordion]
