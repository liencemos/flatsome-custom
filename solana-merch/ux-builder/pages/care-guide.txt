[/col]
[col span="7" span__sm="12" class="edit-page-panel"]
[ux_text]
<p class="edit-label">01 / COTTON JERSEY</p><h2>Printed tees.</h2>
<p>Wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Hang dry and avoid ironing directly over the print.</p>
<hr>
<p class="edit-label">02 / COTTON TWILL</p><h2>Embroidered caps.</h2>
<p>Spot clean with a damp cloth and air dry. Check the garment label before washing.</p>
